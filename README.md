\# RecoverAI 🚀



\### AI-Powered Revenue Recovery \& Cash-Flow Intelligence Platform



RecoverAI is an AI-powered revenue recovery agent designed to help merchants recover revenue lost through failed payments, abandoned checkouts, overdue invoices, and other at-risk transactions.



Instead of simply detecting failed payments, RecoverAI analyzes transaction behavior, determines the safest recovery strategy, applies guardrails, executes permitted recovery actions, and estimates the impact on the merchant's future cash flow.



\---



\## 🎯 Problem



Small and medium-sized merchants can lose significant revenue because of:



\- Failed payments

\- Payment retries that are never attempted

\- Abandoned transactions

\- Overdue payments

\- Subscription payment failures

\- Recovery actions that require manual intervention



Traditional payment systems identify failures but often leave the merchant to decide what to do next.



RecoverAI turns this process into an intelligent recovery workflow.
